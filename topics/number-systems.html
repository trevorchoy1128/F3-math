① 點數咭① Dot cards

每張咭上的點數，就是那個位的位值。按咭翻開（1）或蓋上（0），數數看得見多少粒點。The number of dots on each card is the place value of that place. Click a card to turn it over (1) or cover it (0), and count how many dots you can see.

② 位值表② Place value table

輸入一個數，看看每個數字代表多少。切換進制時，會換成同一個數的另一種寫法。Enter a number to see what each digit stands for. Switching the number system shows the same number written the other way.

③ 逢十進一 vs 逢二進一③ Carry at ten vs carry at two

按「+1」逐一數數，比較兩種進制何時進位。有變化的格會亮起。Press “+1” to count up one at a time and compare when each system carries over. Digits that change light up.

④ 糖果包裝工廠④ Candy packing factory

工廠規定：每 2 粒糖包成 1 袋，每 2 袋裝成 1 盒，每 2 盒裝成 1 箱，每 2 箱裝成 1 大箱。包裝完後，每種包裝各有多少個？Factory rules: every 2 candies go into 1 bag, every 2 bags into 1 box, every 2 boxes into 1 crate, and every 2 crates into 1 large crate. After packing, how many of each package are there?
