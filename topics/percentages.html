② 單利息 vs 複利息② Simple vs compound interest

同樣的本金和利率，單利息每年只按本金計利息；複利息則按上一年的本利和計利息。With the same principal and interest rate, simple interest is calculated on the principal every year; compound interest is calculated on the amount at the end of the previous year.
